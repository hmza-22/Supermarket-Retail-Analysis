# Supermarket-Retail-Analysis
Supermart Grocery Sales Dashboard  An interactive Excel dashboard that analyzes Supermart’s grocery sales data and gives management a single place to understand sales, profit, products, discounts, and geographic performance, without having to dig through the full dataset.

Project Overview

Management needed a simple, easy-to-read view of how the grocery business is performing. This project converts raw sales data into an interactive dashboard built in Microsoft Excel, using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and Slicers to answer key business questions.

Business Objectives

1. Overall Sales Performance

Total sales and total profit
Share of sales by product category
Sales trend over time
Top-performing regions by sales

2. Product Performance

Categories and sub-categories with the highest sales
Products with high sales but comparatively low profit

3. Profit and Discount Analysis

Categories and regions generating the highest profit
Relationship between discount levels and profit

4. Geographic Performance

States and cities with the highest sales
Locations with high sales but comparatively low profit
Key Features
Summary KPIs for total sales and total profit
Category-wise sales contribution
Time-based sales trend analysis
Region, state, and city performance breakdown
Discount vs. profit relationship analysis
Interactive filtering through slicers
Tools and Techniques
Microsoft Excel
Data cleaning and preparation
Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts
Slicers for interactivity
Dashboard design and data visualization
